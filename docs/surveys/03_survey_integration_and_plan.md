# Compression, Explanation, and Cross-Country Robustness: An Integration Survey and Expansion Plan for an Edge-Deployed TB Severity Model (Survey C, v2)

> **Scope and stance.** This document integrates Survey A (XAI for medical imaging, with an edge lens) and Survey B (edge/low-compute deployment) with a third body of recent *compression × robustness* research, around one non-negotiable axis: **any edge compression must be re-validated under the patient-disjoint Leave-One-Country-Out (LOCO) protocol and must not erode either the cross-country generalization gap or the conformal coverage that underpins the project's headline novelty.** This is v2; it replaces the first-pass v1, makes the no-compromise-LOCO constraint the central design axis rather than a closing caveat, and applies the six critic fixes (conflation fix, distillation falsification protocol, WS6 = CORAL × quantization, deepened composite-score conformal WS5, energy as a first-class Pareto axis, factual correction RAD-DINO = 838,336 CXRs). Latency/memory figures imported from Survey B carry their regime caveats (batch-1, fixed 518×518) wherever they enter the mapping table.
>
> **System under study.** Frozen RAD-DINO encoder (DINOv2 ViT-B/14, image-only self-distilled on **838,336 (~838k) CXRs** [Pérez-García 2024]; 518×518 → 768-d CLS + 37×37 patch grid avg-pooled to 7×7 = 49 tokens) with two lightweight heads on frozen features: (a) an ALP-regression MLP (Balanced-MSE) with a rung ladder = kNN retrieval calibration, transductive CORAL test-time feature alignment, deep-ensemble (M=10) + split-conformal; (b) a spatial-attention cavity head scoring each of 49 patches, softmax-pooled (the attention map is the built-in explanation, concentrating on upper lobes). Target output: Timika severity = ALP + 40·1[cavity present], range 0–140. Evaluation: LOCO on TB Portals (Romania / Moldova / Kazakhstan). Calibration symptoms: split-conformal nominal 0.90 **under-covers Moldova at 0.83**; calibration slope < 1 (0.55–0.68) → severe band [100,140] under-predicted.

## Part C1 — The Interconnection: XAI ↔ Edge ↔ Cross-country robustness

The four desiderata of a deployable LMIC TB tool — **faithful explanation**, **valid uncertainty**, **on-device efficiency**, and **cross-country (LOCO) robustness** — are usually studied in three disjoint literatures (Survey A, Survey B, and the new compression-robustness research). They are not independent. Compression is the operation that couples them: it is undertaken for efficiency, but it perturbs the encoder's decision function, and that perturbation lands hardest on exactly the second-order properties — tail behaviour, calibration, coverage, and explanation faithfulness — that the other three desiderata depend on. The governing empirical fact across the new research is that **top-line accuracy hides what compression destroys, and the damage is selective: it falls on the rare, shifted, tail-distribution cases that define OOD generalization** [Hooker 2019; Hooker 2020; Liebenwein 2021]. For a system whose entire value is a *cross-country* shift gap plus *per-country* conformal coverage, this is not a footnote; it is the central design risk.

### C1.1 Explanation faithfulness under compression — does the cavity attention survive?

The cavity head's softmax-attention map *is* the clinical explanation, and Survey A already establishes two hard constraints on it before any compression enters: raw attention is not automatically faithful (the Jain–Wallace vs Wiegreffe–Pinter debate, [Jain 2019; Wiegreffe 2019]; the Faithfulness Violation Test [Liu 2022]; SaCo showing many ViT-explanation metrics cannot beat Random Attribution [Wu 2024]), and DINOv2-class encoders carry high-norm artifact tokens that leak into pooled maps [Darcet 2024]. Compression adds a third, under-studied threat. **Token reduction** is the single highest-leverage latency optimization (it attacks the actual 1369-token O(N²) cost; DINOv2 is unusually prune-tolerant — OATS reports only −0.41% top-1 at 50% compression on DINOv2-Giant [Stephen Zhang 2024], and ToMe doubles ViT-L/H throughput at 0.2–0.3% accuracy drop [Bolya 2023]) — but it *changes the 37×37 grid*, directly threatening the 49-token attention map. The OOD literature treats *accuracy*, not *explanation faithfulness*, as the output, so the effect of token merging on a clinically meaningful localization map is essentially unmeasured (Survey A open problem #6; Survey B gap #2). **Quantization** behaves as a low-pass spectral regularizer that bins away fine-grained, high-frequency features [Bouguerra 2025]; the same mechanism that mildly helps synthetic-noise robustness could blur the sharp upper-lobe cavity signal. **Distillation** does not transfer attention structure for free — a 2024 result shows copying a teacher ViT's attention maps recovers most of pretraining's benefit [A. Li 2024], implying the converse: a student that does *not* explicitly match attention may lose the explanation even at accuracy parity. The integration conclusion: explanation fidelity must be measured *as a function of compression budget* — attention-map IoU / rank-correlation / EBPG [Wang 2020] against the FP32 map and against TB Portals sextant cavity labels — at every step, never assumed (this is white-space **WS1**).

### C1.2 The interpretability–efficiency trade-off, and why the frozen backbone resolves it

Survey A's organizing constraint is the faithfulness–latency Pareto frontier: faithful explainers (Chefer relevance [Chefer 2021], AttnLRP [Achtibat 2024], CDAM [Brocki 2024], RISE [Petsiuk 2018], Score-CAM [Wang 2020]) need gradients, smoothing, or many forward passes, while the cheapest built-in attention is the least faithful. The frozen-encoder-plus-cheap-head archetype resolves this elegantly: the backbone forward is the only unavoidable cost, so a class-specific, signed upgrade of the raw attention map via CDAM/Chefer costs **one backward pass through the tiny head**, trivially cheap even on CPU. Compression *re-opens* the trade-off, because it changes the very features the explanation reads. The integration insight is that **the interpretability–efficiency trade-off and the robustness–efficiency trade-off are the same trade-off viewed twice**: both are governed by whether compression preserves the fine-grained, tail-sensitive features (cavity texture, upper-lobe localization, worst-country exemplars) or erases them in favour of low-frequency, shortcut-prone features [Bouguerra 2025; Hooker 2019]. A compression recipe that preserves the LOCO gap will, by the same mechanism, tend to preserve the explanation; one that amplifies shortcut reliance will tend to break both.

### C1.3 Uncertainty under compression — cheap edge UQ, and the conformal validity question (CONFLATION FIX)

**Cheap UQ is nearly free here, by architecture.** All per-member cost lives in the lightweight ALP/cavity heads on one cached 768-d CLS + 49-token feature, so the M=10 deep ensemble [Lakshminarayanan 2017] need not be sacrificed: run the ViT forward once, ensemble the cheap heads (Survey A's "ensemble only the heads, not the backbone"). If even that is too much, **last-layer Laplace** [Daxberger 2021] is essentially free and fits a frozen backbone exactly, and **Packed-Ensembles** [Laurent 2023] explicitly preserve deep-ensemble shift robustness within a single net's budget. The expensive ViT-B is what gets compressed; UQ richness does not have to be traded for it.

**Conformal validity under compression — the precise statement (CONFLATION FIX).** v1 muddled two distinct things; v2 separates them.

1. *In-distribution coverage under quantization holds by construction — but only with the calibrate-after-quantize qualifier.* Split-conformal coverage is finite-sample and model-agnostic [Angelopoulos 2021]: it holds under **exchangeability of calibration and test points through the same score function**. Therefore, if calibration scores are **recomputed with the quantized model** on a fresh exchangeable calibration set, INT8 in-distribution coverage is preserved by construction. The *silent failure mode* is the opposite order: **FP32-calibrate-then-deploy-quantized** changes the score function (quantization shifts the model's own output distribution [Ferianc 2021; Proskurina 2024]), breaking exchangeability between the FP32 calibration quantile and the INT8 test scores, and coverage drifts. This is a concrete pipeline rule, not a theorem about quantization being benign: *recalibrate conformal scores after every compression step.*
2. *LOCO cross-country shift already breaks exchangeability — independently of quantization.* Held-out-country calibration and test points are not exchangeable with the training pool regardless of bit-width; this is why Moldova under-covers at 0.83 in FP32. The tools are **weighted CP** [Tibshirani 2019], **conformal prediction beyond exchangeability** [Barber 2023], and **Mondrian / per-country CP** [Ding 2023] — none of which is about compression.
3. *The genuinely new, unstudied question is the INTERACTION.* Does INT8/INT4 quantization noise *worsen the already-fragile cross-country coverage*? Quantization damage concentrates on OOD inputs (GPTQ calibration-set overfitting disproportionately harms OOD [Proskurina 2024]) — i.e., on exactly the held-out country — and quantization can amplify shortcut reliance (+2.7% at W8A8, +12.5% at W4A8 [Bouguerra 2025]), the precise mechanism that re-opens a cross-country gap. No published work measures whether stacking quantization-induced output shift *on top of* cross-country covariate shift compounds a coverage gap. This is **WS5** (deepened below). The honest stance: do **not** state "coverage holds by construction" without the *in-distribution-only* qualifier, and treat compounded-shift coverage as an empirical, gated quantity.

### C1.4 CENTRAL: compression vs OOD/cross-domain robustness & calibration — does shrinking the encoder erode the LOCO gap?

This is the axis the whole document turns on, and the new research speaks to it directly and consistently.

- **The damage is selective and tail-concentrated.** Pruning/quantization preserve top-1/top-5 while disproportionately harming a small long-tail subset — Pruning Identified Exemplars (PIEs) — of atypical, underrepresented examples [Hooker 2019]; compression *amplifies* error disparities on minority subgroups (Compression Identified Exemplars, CIE) rather than distributing loss uniformly [Hooker 2020]. The worst-held-out-country exemplars that *define* the LOCO gap are precisely such a tail; pooled accuracy can hold while Moldova quietly degrades.
- **i.i.d. validation is actively misleading.** Pruned nets degrade on OOD generalization, noise resilience, and corruption robustness at prune ratios that look fine on the i.i.d. test set — test accuracy is an insufficient stopping criterion [Liebenwein 2021]. In medical imaging specifically, i.i.d. AUC parity hides OOD collapse: ImageNet-pretrained models match RadImageNet i.i.d. but collapse far worse under a CT-tag confounder (OOD AUC 0.02 vs 0.22) [Juodelyte 2024]. Domain-pretraining (RAD-DINO is CXR-pretrained on 838k CXRs) *buys* the OOD robustness that compression must not erode.
- **The effect is not monotone — it is recipe-, architecture-, and sparsity-dependent.** Quantization improves *synthetic*-noise robustness (+8.9% avg, low-pass regularizer) but *degrades semantic/natural* distribution-shift robustness (ImageNet-A/R/Sketch) and increases shortcut reliance [Bouguerra 2025]. Moderate-sparsity Dynamic Sparse Training can *beat* dense on corruption robustness via implicit low-frequency regularization [arXiv:2410.03030]. Across CNNs on CIFAR-C, ~69% of compressed configs matched/beat the dense mCE but the spread was enormous (best mCE 76.7 vs worst 201.7 from naive int8 QAT) — *recipe choice dominates the robustness outcome* [arXiv:2512.24971, CIFAR-10 figures]. The lesson: robustness is a *selection criterion* for the compression recipe, not an afterthought.
- **Calibration moves unpredictably under compression** — ECE can improve OR worsen sharply depending on pretraining-data quality (curated WIT models gained; noisy-web LAION up to +49% worse; text-encoder PTQ +98%, recoverable with QAT) [Bouguerra 2025]; reliability degrades faster than accuracy as compression increases [Misra 2023, partly_confirmed — used only for the general "reliability ≠ accuracy parity" framing]. Frozen ViT backbones may calibrate worse than CNNs on medical data [Hekler 2025, first clause only], strengthening the case for explicit post-hoc temperature scaling [Guo 2017] + conformal after compression.
- **Distillation does NOT transfer robustness for free.** Students often fail to match teacher predictive distributions even with capacity — an optimization, not capacity, limit; fidelity ≠ generalization [Stanton 2021]. Robustness transfer must be *engineered*: Discrete Adversarial Distillation distills OOD robustness from a robust CLIP teacher via VQGAN-discretized adversarial examples [Zhou 2023]; Robust Soft-Label Distillation shows robustness transfers through soft labels (the teacher's robust decision geometry), not hard labels [Zi 2021]; calibrated-teacher KD transfers calibration only when the teacher is itself calibrated [Yang 2021; Kim 2025].
- **The frozen design is already the OOD-optimal substrate.** Full fine-tuning distorts pretrained features and underperforms OOD (+2% ID but −7% OOD vs linear probing across 10 shifts; LP-FT recovers both) [Kumar 2022]. The frozen-RAD-DINO + light-heads design is therefore *defensively* compressed against the worst gap-eroder — fine-tuning — which narrows the safe-compression search space to (a) the encoder's inference precision/token count and (b) the trivially-cheap heads.

**Recommended compression order (lowest LOCO risk first), as an integration synthesis:** (1) token *merging* (not hard dropping) inside the ViT-B [Bolya 2023] — biggest latency win, prune-tolerant family, but gated on attention-map preservation; (2) INT8 PTQ of the frozen encoder [Yuan 2022; Li 2023a; Ferianc 2021 supports INT8 over sub-8-bit for UQ survival] with recalibrate-after-quantize; (3) sub-8-bit only with Lipschitz-controlled / Defensive-Quantization-style error-amplification control [Lin 2019] and full LOCO re-validation; (4) DG-aware pruning that ranks channels/tokens by *cross-country invariance* (variance of domain-level risk) rather than magnitude [Cai 2022], if pruning at all; (5) distillation only via a robustness/calibration-targeted recipe [Zhou 2023; Zi 2021], with the falsification protocol of C3/C4.

### C1.5 The publishable white space (WS1–WS6)

- **WS1 — Explanation faithfulness vs compression budget.** No standard metric certifies that a compressed model's attention/explanation survives. For a system whose attention map *is* the cavity explanation, "explanation fidelity (attention-map IoU / EBPG vs FP32 and vs sextant labels) as a function of token-budget / bit-width / distillation" is an acute, unmeasured trade-off (Survey A #6; Survey B #2). Token merging's effect on patch-level explanations is essentially unstudied [Bolya 2023; gaps in rr-ood-robustness].
- **WS2 — Per-country conformal coverage under compression (compound shift).** Whether INT8/INT4 worsens the already-fragile Moldova 0.83 coverage is unmeasured for medical imaging under cross-site shift; nobody has characterized quantization-induced output shift *stacked on* cross-country covariate shift (rr-calib-uq gaps 1–2). The novel object is per-country coverage at each bit-width vs the FP32 M=10 baseline.
- **WS3 — Regression-native, LOCO-aware compression & TTA.** Every major TTA method (TENT/EATA/SAR/CoTTA/MEMO [Wang 2021; Niu 2022; Niu 2023; Wang 2022]) is built on classification entropy/pseudo-labels; there is no entropy analog for a Balanced-MSE ALP regressor + attention-pooled cavity head. Compression's effect on a *continuous* target (no softmax to absorb error; the severe-band [100,140] tail) is largely unstudied (Survey B #3; rr-ood-robustness gap 1).
- **WS4 — Deep-ensemble diversity under correlated compression error.** Does compressing each of M=10 ensemble members (or the shared frozen backbone they sit on) preserve the uncertainty diversity that underpins conformal width, or does correlated quantization error collapse it? Unexamined (rr-calib-uq gap 3, rr-ood-robustness gap 4). Cheap-ensemble shift robustness is benchmarked on CIFAR/ImageNet-C, not on frozen-foundation heads under medical LOCO.
- **WS5 — Composite-score conformal under compression and shift (DEEPENED).** Timika = ALP (continuous) + 40·1[cavity] (Bernoulli) is a **sum of heterogeneous predictors**, so its conformal interval is a *convolution/mixture, not a simple interval*. No published conformal method targets this exact form: symmetric-calibration interval arithmetic handles sums of *homogeneous* conformalized labels [Luo 2024]; the heterogeneous regression+thresholded-binary mixture, with the binary entering via a learned threshold, is unaddressed (rr-composite gap 1). The constructive proposal (C4) is CQR for ALP [Romano 2019] + a calibrated/conformal classifier for cavity, composed via symmetric-calibration interval arithmetic [Luo 2024] (with Bonferroni-of-monotone as a provable fallback, exact at the endpoints because f(a,c)=a+40c is monotone in both), and **conformal risk control [Angelopoulos 2022] / RCPS [Bates 2021] / Learn-then-Test** layered on top to guarantee a derived clinical risk (cavity false-negative rate, or missed-severe Timika≥100 rate). The *new* twist: do all of this *under compression and per-country*, where the held-out country supplies no calibration split (rr-composite gap 2).
- **WS6 — CORAL × quantization order-of-operations (NEW, first-class).** Transductive CORAL re-standardizes test-country features to train-pool statistics at test time. If the encoder is quantized, *on which features are the CORAL covariance statistics computed?* The decisive evidence is that **computing feature statistics on already-quantized features is numerically unstable and degrades alignment**, because quantization discretizes the continuous feature distribution that statistical alignment assumes [Deng 2025]; the fix is gradient-free / quantization-aware scale adaptation rather than backprop on quantized activations. This is *worse* for CORAL (second-order covariance) than for BN (means). The unstudied, load-bearing rule: **compute the CORAL whitening/coloring transform from higher-precision (pre-quantization or de-quantized) encoder features, then apply it; or fold alignment into a quantization-aware calibration step.** This interaction is more load-bearing for THIS system than the federated item v1 gave a full P2 slot, and it is a cheap experiment (Part C3, P1).

## Part C3 — Prioritised expansion roadmap (P0 / P1 / P2)

Every compression step below carries an explicit **LOCO RE-VALIDATION GATE** as an acceptance criterion: report, per held-out country, Timika MAE *and* ALP MAE, the **cross-country GAP** (worst-country minus pooled), per-country split-conformal coverage at nominal 0.90 (Mondrian), calibration slope, and severe-band [100,140] error — versus the FP32 M=10 baseline. A step ships only if (a) the gap does not widen and (b) worst-country coverage stays within tolerance of 0.90. Because the backbone is frozen, features cache once and the entire ablation ladder trains in ~6 T4-GPU-hours, so these gates are cheap to re-run.

### P0 — Foundational baselines and instrumentation (free-tier T4; ~6 GPU-h/ladder)

1. **Establish the FP32 LOCO + conformal baseline panel.** Lock the reporting object: per-country Timika/ALP MAE, gap, Mondrian per-country coverage, calibration slope, severe-band error. *Compute:* T4. *Expected:* reproduces the known Moldova 0.83 under-coverage and slope 0.55–0.68. *Null/risk:* none — this is the measuring stick everything else is gated against.
2. **CQR + composite-score conformal (WS5).** Replace constant-width residual conformal on ALP with CQR [Romano 2019]; conformalize the cavity head as a {0,40} set; compose via symmetric-calibration interval arithmetic [Luo 2024] with Bonferroni-of-monotone fallback. Add CRC/RCPS [Angelopoulos 2022; Bates 2021] for a cavity-FNR / missed-severe certificate. *Compute:* T4 (post-hoc on cached features). *Expected:* adaptive width narrows the severe-band under-coverage; tighter composite interval than naive addition. *Null/risk:* CQR adaptivity limited by quantile-fit quality; gains over Bonferroni shrink for a 2-term monotone sum (still the right baseline).
3. **Per-country (Mondrian) + weighted/clustered CP as the LOCO coverage tool.** [Tibshirani 2019; Barber 2023; Ding 2023]. *Expected:* formal per-country coverage; clustered-CP recovers Moldova's small-calibration variance. *Null/risk:* held-out country has *no* calibration split — must combine with weighted CP / leave-country-out calibration (this is itself a contribution, C4).
4. **Validate the cavity attention as a faithful explanation (Survey A battery, pre-compression).** EBPG [Wang 2020] against sextant labels as primary; MPRT [Adebayo 2018] (randomize head weights → map degrades); token-level deletion/insertion with ROAD imputation [Rong 2022]; attention-map repeatability across ensemble members [Wiegreffe 2019]; upgrade to class-specific signed map via CDAM/Chefer [Brocki 2024; Chefer 2021] (one head-backward). *Compute:* T4. *Expected:* establishes the FP32 faithfulness baseline that WS1 measures against. *Null/risk:* raw attention may fail MPRT or FVT [Liu 2022] — if so, the signed CDAM upgrade becomes load-bearing.

### P1 — Robustness-aware compression with LOCO gates (free-tier T4 mostly; A100 helpful for distillation)

5. **INT8 PTQ of the frozen encoder, recalibrate-after-quantize (CONFLATION FIX in practice).** RepQ-ViT/SmoothQuant outlier handling [Li 2023a; Xiao 2023], 32 in-domain CXR calibration images; recompute all conformal quantiles with the INT8 model. **LOCO GATE.** *Compute:* T4. *Expected:* ~4× size, INT8 latency win on ANE/Hexagon/WASM, LOCO gap and (recalibrated) per-country coverage within tolerance. *Null/risk:* Moldova coverage regresses (the WS2 signal); shortcut amplification widens the gap [Bouguerra 2025] — then fall back to QAT-style recovery or keep encoder INT8 + heads FP.
6. **WS6 — CORAL × quantization order experiment.** Compare three orderings: (a) CORAL covariance on FP32 features → apply, then quantize; (b) CORAL on quantized features [the unstable case Deng 2025 warns of]; (c) quantization-aware / de-quantized covariance. **LOCO GATE on held-out-country MAE + coverage.** *Compute:* T4 (cheap — alignment is gradient-free, batch-level). *Expected:* (a)/(c) preserve the gap; (b) mis-aligns the held-out country and widens it. *Null/risk:* if quantized-feature covariance turns out robust at INT8, the rule relaxes to INT8 — still a publishable negative-or-positive measurement.
7. **Token merging (ToMe/ATM) inside the ViT-B with WS1 explanation gate.** [Bolya 2023; Lee 2025], proportional-attention bookkeeping, prefer merge over drop. **GATE: attention-map IoU/EBPG vs FP32 and vs sextant labels, in addition to the LOCO panel.** *Compute:* T4 (training-free). *Expected:* ~2× throughput at ≤0.5% ID drop; map survives if merging is proportional. *Null/risk:* merging blurs the upper-lobe cavity localization → cap the merge ratio at the largest budget that keeps EBPG within tolerance.
8. **WS4 — deep-ensemble diversity under shared-backbone compression.** Measure whether INT8/merged backbone collapses the M=10 head-ensemble's predictive diversity (and thus conformal width); compare to Packed/last-layer-Laplace substitutes [Laurent 2023; Daxberger 2021]. *Compute:* T4. *Expected:* diversity largely preserved (cheap heads dominate variance); if not, switch to Packed head-ensemble. *Null/risk:* correlated quantization error narrows intervals → spurious over-confidence, a coverage failure the gate catches.

### P2 — Distillation (the flagship; A100 obtainable) and TTA

9. **Distillation to a ViT-S/14 (or EfficientFormerV2-S0) student WITH the FALSIFICATION PROTOCOL (replaces v1's unfounded assertion).** Re-run DINOv2 iBOT with the frozen RAD-DINO teacher [Oquab 2023]; SEED/DisCo embedding matching + per-patch attention matching to preserve the 49-token grid and the explanation [Fang 2021; Gao 2022; A. Li 2024]; robustness-targeted objective [Zhou 2023; Zi 2021]; re-fit the calibration ladder on student features.

   **DISTILLATION FALSIFICATION PROTOCOL — isolate WHERE the cross-country robustness comes from (teacher-features vs distillation-data):**
   - (a) **Student distilled from the RAD-DINO teacher** (the proposed model).
   - (b) **Same student architecture trained from scratch on TB Portals** (no distillation) — isolates whether the gap comes from the data alone.
   - (c) **Student distilled from an ImageNet / natural-image teacher** — isolates whether *CXR-domain teacher features* (not just "distillation") carry the gap; [Juodelyte 2024] predicts (c) collapses under cross-country shift.
   - (d) **Ablate distillation-data scale** (fraction of the 838k-CXR-style corpus / TB Portals) — isolates data-scale dependence.
   - (e) **Linear-probe the student features under LOCO** — tests whether the *representation* (not the head) carries the cross-country invariance.

   **Acceptance:** (a) preserves the gap and per-country coverage within tolerance of FP32 RAD-DINO; (a) > (b) and (a) > (c) on the gap proves teacher-features (CXR-domain) are the source, not generic distillation or data. **LOCO GATE on every variant.** *Compute:* iBOT re-run benefits from an **A100**; the linear-probe/ablation ladder runs on T4 (cached features). *Expected:* (a)≈teacher, (b) wider gap, (c) much wider gap, (a) preserves attention map if attention-matched. *Plausible null/risk:* the gap may turn out to come mostly from the *data* (b≈a), which would *refute* the "teacher-features preserve the gap" claim — this is exactly the falsifiable outcome the protocol is designed to surface, and either result is publishable.

10. **Regression-native TTA on edge (WS3), composed with quantization correctly.** Adapt a LeanTTA-style backprop-free, stateless, BS=1 quantized TTA [Cai 2025] to the regression heads + CORAL, using the WS6 precision rule; benchmark against the medical-TTA caution that no paradigm wins everywhere [MedSeg-TTA 2025]. **LOCO GATE; also check TTA does not break conformal exchangeability** (rr-robust-compress-tta gap 6). *Compute:* T4. *Expected:* recovers held-out-country MAE on-device within the 11.2 MB / near-inference-latency envelope. *Null/risk:* test-time adaptation can violate the exchangeability split-CP assumes → pair with QTC-style unlabeled recalibration [Yilmaz & Heckel 2022] and re-measure coverage.

## Part C4 — Candidate paper contributions

Each contribution states the gap, the experiment + baselines + LOCO-aware metrics, honest feasibility on our compute, and a target venue. **The flagship is Contribution 1.**

**Contribution 1 (FLAGSHIP) — Compression that provably preserves the cross-country generalization gap + faithful on-device explanation.**
*Gap:* No work measures whether compression of a frozen medical foundation encoder preserves a *country-level LOCO* gap and *per-country conformal coverage* (rr-ood-robustness gaps 2,3; rr-calib-uq gap 1; Survey B #4). *Experiment:* the P1 ladder — INT8 PTQ (recalibrate-after-quantize) + token merging + WS6 CORAL-precision rule — each gated on the LOCO panel and the WS1 explanation-fidelity panel; the P2 distillation flagship with its falsification protocol. *Baselines:* FP32 M=10 RAD-DINO; naive accuracy-only compression (the predicted-to-fail recipe [Hooker 2019; Liebenwein 2021]); Kantipudi et al. DenseNet Timika baseline (MAE 13.36–14.01% of range [Kantipudi 2024]). *Metrics:* per-country Timika/ALP MAE, cross-country gap, Mondrian per-country coverage, calibration slope, severe-band error, attention-map EBPG/IoU vs FP32 and sextant labels, plus latency/size/memory/**energy-per-inference** across the four backends. *Feasibility:* high — frozen features → ~6 T4-h per ladder; A100 only helps the iBOT distillation. *Venue:* MICCAI / IEEE TMI / ICONIP 2026 main track.

**Contribution 2 — Composite-score conformal for a heterogeneous clinical sum, under per-country shift (WS5).**
*Gap:* No conformal method targets Timika = ALP + 40·1[cavity], a regression + thresholded-Bernoulli sum, *and* delivers per-country coverage where the held-out country has no calibration split (rr-composite gaps 1–2). *Experiment:* CQR(ALP) ⊕ conformal cavity classifier composed via symmetric-calibration interval arithmetic [Luo 2024] + Bonferroni-of-monotone fallback; Mondrian + weighted/clustered CP for per-country coverage; CRC/RCPS [Angelopoulos 2022; Bates 2021] for a cavity-FNR / missed-severe certificate. *Baselines:* constant-width split-CP; naive added per-component intervals. *Metrics:* composite-interval coverage + width per country, severe-band coverage, certified cavity-FNR. *Feasibility:* high — post-hoc on cached features, T4. *Venue:* NeurIPS/ICML workshop on distribution-free UQ, or a methods-leaning medical venue.

**Contribution 3 — CORAL × quantization order-of-operations for test-time cross-country alignment (WS6).**
*Gap:* The interaction of transductive covariance alignment with a quantized encoder is unstudied; [Deng 2025] shows statistics-on-quantized-features is unstable but covers BN, not CORAL. *Experiment:* the P1 three-ordering experiment (FP32-covariance-then-quantize vs covariance-on-quantized vs quantization-aware). *Baselines:* no alignment; BN-style alignment. *Metrics:* held-out-country MAE + per-country coverage at each ordering and bit-width. *Feasibility:* very high — gradient-free, batch-level, T4, fits the LeanTTA envelope [Cai 2025]. *Venue:* edge-ML / efficient-DL workshop or a TMI short paper. *(A clean, cheap, decisive result — strong companion to the flagship.)*

**Contribution 4 — Explanation-fidelity-vs-compression-budget for a clinical attention map (WS1).**
*Gap:* Token reduction / quantization / distillation are optimized for FLOPs/accuracy; none certifies the surviving attention map is still clinically faithful (Survey A #6; Survey B #2; rr-robust-compress-tta gap 4). *Experiment:* sweep token-merge ratio / bit-width / distillation and report attention-map IoU + EBPG vs FP32 and vs TB Portals sextant cavity labels, alongside the LOCO panel. *Baselines:* FP32 attention map; randomized-head map (MPRT [Adebayo 2018]). *Metrics:* EBPG/IoU/Pointing-Game sweep, MPRT pass/fail, token-deletion logit-drop [Rong 2022]. *Feasibility:* high — cached features, T4. *Venue:* iMIMIC / interpretable-ML medical workshop, or folded into the flagship as a section.

**Contribution 5 — A LOCO-and-coverage-gated compression protocol as a reusable methodology.**
*Gap:* The field validates compression on i.i.d. test sets, which is actively misleading for OOD-valued systems [Liebenwein 2021; Juodelyte 2024]; there is no standard "ship only if the gap and per-country coverage hold" gate. *Experiment:* formalize the gate (with the CIE-style check [Hooker 2020] — surface exemplars where compressed and full models disagree most and verify they are not concentrated in the held-out country) and demonstrate it catching a naive-PTQ Moldova-coverage regression that pooled MAE hides. *Baselines:* i.i.d.-only validation. *Metrics:* the full LOCO panel + CIE concentration-by-country. *Feasibility:* high, T4. *Venue:* a position/benchmark track (e.g., a reproducibility or trustworthy-ML venue).

**Contribution 6 (stretch) — Distillation falsification: where does the cross-country gap come from?**
*Gap:* v1-style claims that a distilled student "preserves the LOCO gap" are untested as to *source* (teacher CXR-features vs distillation-data vs scale); [Stanton 2021] warns robustness is not inherited. *Experiment:* the P2 falsification protocol (variants a–e, linear-probe under LOCO). *Baselines:* from-scratch student (b), ImageNet-teacher student (c). *Metrics:* per-country gap and coverage across variants; linear-probe LOCO MAE. *Feasibility:* iBOT re-run benefits from an A100 (obtainable); ablations on T4. *Venue:* main-track if the result is clean (especially if it *refutes* the easy assumption), otherwise an SSL/distillation workshop.
