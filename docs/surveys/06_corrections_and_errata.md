## 1. RAD-DINO training-set size — Survey B, §1, line 7 (and Survey A, §4.4, line 84)

**Location:** `02_survey_edge.md` §1 Introduction ("trained image-only on ~883k CXRs from five public datasets"); also `01_survey_xai.md` §4.4 ("continually pretrained on ~840k CXRs (the paper's figure; the public checkpoint is ~834k…)").

**What is wrong:**
- Survey B states the model was trained on "~883k CXRs from five public datasets." The authoritative figure in the paper (Pérez-García et al. 2024, arXiv:2401.10815) is **838,336 CXRs** (from 439,177 subjects), and that total **includes a ~90,000-image private dataset** — so it is *not* "five public datasets." ~883k is overstated relative to the paper.

The "~834k" and "lateral views" claims are not supported.

**Correct statement (source):** Per the RAD-DINO paper (Pérez-García et al., *Exploring Scalable Medical Image Encoders Beyond Text Supervision*, arXiv:2401.10815 / Nat. Mach. The publicly released HF checkpoint (microsoft/rad-dino) was instead trained on **882,775 images from the five public datasets only** ("different from the one in the paper, where some private data was used"). Reconcile the headline figure to **838,336 (~838k)**.
